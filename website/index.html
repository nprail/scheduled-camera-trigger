A camera trigger for the exact moment.

Kairos is a time-based camera trigger. It wakes your camera and starts and stops recording at the second you choose. You set it from your phone.

View on GitHub Read the docs

Built to record a rocket launch without anyone next to the camera. It does one thing: be ready, and press record at the right second.

Features

Everything you need to hit the moment.

  • Exact timing

    Plan each attempt around one moment, T-0. Kairos wakes the camera, starts recording before it and stops after it. Plan as many attempts as you need.

  • Works with your camera

    Works with cameras that have a remote trigger port, and with Z CAM cameras.

  • Controlled from your phone

    The iPhone and Android apps connect to Kairos over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). Edit the schedule, check the countdown, run a test and watch the log, in the field.

  • Small and low power

    Small enough to sit next to the camera, and light on power. A small battery pack is enough.

  • Open

    The device software and both apps are open source and documented in one repository.

How it works

Set it up once. Then walk away.

  1. 01

    Set the attempts

    Add your T-0 times, then set three timings: when to wake the camera, when to start recording, and when to stop.

  2. 02

    Run a test

    Run a test from the app to check that everything works with your camera.

  3. 03

    Leave

    Walk away. Kairos wakes the camera and starts and stops recording on schedule.

Apps

For iPhone and Android.

Set up and control Kairos from your phone. You can:

  • Find and connect to devices, and pair with the button
  • Show live status with a countdown to the next event
  • Edit the schedule and the camera settings
  • Run a test, and wake, start, stop and sleep the camera by hand
  • Show the device log, live

Download

Get the app.

iPhone

Build it yourself

Not on the App Store. You build it with Xcode and install it on your own iPhone.

Install on iPhone
  1. On a Mac, install Xcode 16 or later, and get the source: 
    git clone https://github.com/nprail/kairos.git
open kairos/apps/ios/Kairos.xcodeproj
  2. In Signing & Capabilities, choose your team (a free Apple ID works) and change the bundle identifier to one that is unique to you.
  3. Connect an iPhone running iOS 17 or later, and turn on Developer Mode in Settings, Privacy & Security.
  4. Choose your iPhone in Xcode and press Run.

Bluetooth doesn't work in the Simulator, so use a real iPhone. An app signed with a free Apple ID stops opening after 7 days. Run it from Xcode again to renew it.

Hardware

Small, simple hardware.

A small ESP32-based device that sits next to your camera and connects to it. It has one button, and it runs from a small battery pack.

Read the hardware guide
Camera
Connects to your camera, wired.
Phone
Controlled from the iPhone or Android app.
Pairing
One button. Only your phone can control the device.
Power
Starts in seconds and is light on power. A small battery pack is enough.
